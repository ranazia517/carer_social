# CARER — Social Voice & Captions Guide

## Social Tone

**What CARER sounds like:**
- A trusted friend who truly gets it — not a wellness brand, not a helpline
- Warm and honest, never performative
- Calm and steady — we don't hype, alarm, or urgency-bait
- Anti-guilt always: caregivers carry enough. We never add to that weight.

**What CARER never sounds like:**
- Clinical ("symptoms", "diagnosis", "mental health condition", "disorder")
- Pushy or salesy ("unlock", "transform", "supercharge", "just $X/month")
- Toxic-positivity ("you've got this!", "just breathe!")
- Medical ("CARER can help with depression / anxiety / PTSD")

**Critical compliance notes:**
- CARER is **not** a medical device, therapist, or emergency service
- No medical claims — ever
- Always include crisis line note where relevant (see footer copy below)
- App is **not yet live on stores** — use "coming soon" / "early access" language only, never claim it's downloadable

---

## Hashtag Approach

Keep it minimal and human. Avoid clinical hashtags entirely.

**Core set (use 3–5 max):**
`#caregiver` `#caregiverlife` `#familycaregiver` `#caregiverburnout` `#lookingafteranotherperson`

**Supplementary:**
`#mentalwellness` `#selfcompassion` `#youmatter` `#caringforothers` `#caregiving`

**Never use:**
`#mentalhealth` (too broad, algorithmically noisy), `#depression`, `#anxiety`, or any clinical condition hashtag
