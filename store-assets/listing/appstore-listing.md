## Description (≤4000 chars)

```
CARER is a warm, private companion built for the person doing the caring.

If you're looking after a partner, parent, child, or friend — whether you'd call yourself a "carer" or not — there's often nowhere to put the hard feelings. The resentment, the fear, the grief for someone who's still here. CARER is built for exactly that: a place to be heard, to hold the difficult things, and to feel a little less alone at 3am.

──── WHAT CARER DOES ────

A COMPANION THAT LISTENS
Your conversations happen on your own device, offline and private by default. CARER meets you where you are, holds the feeling first, and never hands out advice you didn't ask for.

MEMORY, IN YOUR OWN WORDS
CARER remembers what you tell it, in your own words, and gently offers it back. You never have to start over or re-explain. Your memory is visible, editable, exportable — and always yours.

A PRIVATE SPACE FOR THE UNSAYABLE
A sealed, encrypted room for the thoughts you can't say aloud — the resentment, the anger, the wish it would just stop. Received without judgement, never re-surfaced without your say-so.

ROOM FOR GRIEF, EVEN FOR SOMEONE STILL HERE
Anticipatory grief and ambiguous loss are first-class experiences here — not a sub-topic to get past. CARER holds the "both/and": love and grief together, neither cancelling the other. No forced closure. And if the caring ends, CARER stays — holding the after.

HELP, ONE TAP AWAY — EVEN OFFLINE
A calm, free crisis and safeguarding route lives on every screen. It works with no signal, before sign-in, and never behind a paywall. Four directions of support: your own safety, breaking point, fear of harm to the person you care for, and harm being done to you.

──── WHAT CARER IS NOT ────

CARER is not therapy, counselling, or a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat, assess, or monitor anyone. When something needs a clinician or a crisis service, CARER says so honestly and points you there.

No burnout meters. No scores. No streaks. No guilt-trip notifications. The person you care for is never profiled, tracked, or monitored in CARER.

──── PRIVACY, BY DESIGN ────

• On-device by default — your words don't need to leave your phone.
• Optional encrypted backup — end-to-end encrypted. Even we can't read it.
• Cloud assistance is off unless you turn it on.
• We never sell your data or train AI on it.
• Export or delete everything, any time.

──── FOR CARERS EVERYWHERE ────

Made by Wellnetix Ltd in the United Kingdom. Available to carers worldwide. Crisis resources inside the app are tailored to your country. The website (carerapp.com/safety) covers the UK, the US, and 175+ countries via Find A Helpline.

CARER is for adults aged 18 and over. If you are a young carer under 18, CARER will point you to dedicated young-carer services.

─────────────────────────────
IMPORTANT: CARER is not an emergency or medical service and does not provide medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency services — 999 in the UK · 911 in the US and Canada · 112 across Europe. For crisis resources, visit carerapp.com/safety.
```

## Category

**Primary:** Health & Fitness

**Why Health & Fitness and not Medical:**
The App Store Medical category is for clinical tools — symptom checkers, medication trackers, diagnostic aids. CARER is explicitly non-clinical: it does not diagnose, treat, assess, or monitor anyone. Health & Fitness is the correct home for wellbeing companions. If a Mental Wellness sub-category is available in your territory, select it.

**Secondary (optional):** Lifestyle

## Age Rating / Content Descriptors

**Recommended: 17+**

Reasons (Apple App Store content questionnaire answers):
- **Infrequent/Mild: Mature/Suggestive Themes** — app addresses grief, emotional distress, burnout, and topics carers can't share elsewhere.
- **Infrequent/Mild: Simulated Gambling** — Not applicable (N/A).
- **Infrequent/Mild: Medical/Treatment Information** — Crisis resources (helpline numbers) are surfaced; no clinical advice given.
- **Violence** — No violence in-app. Crisis module references domestic abuse safety planning (text only, no graphic depiction).
- **Infrequent/Mild: Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use** — N/A.
- **Unrestricted Web Access** — No. External links are to crisis helplines and the product website only.

_Note: The app includes references to self-harm ("when you don't want to go on") and domestic abuse as part of the crisis layer. These are handled sensitively and redirected to helplines — but warrant the 17+ rating._
