## Full Description (≤4000 chars)

```
CARER is a warm, private companion built for the person doing the caring.

If you're looking after a partner, parent, child, or friend — whether you'd call yourself a "carer" or not — there's often nowhere to put the hard feelings. The resentment, the fear, the grief for someone who's still here. CARER is built for exactly that: a place to be heard, to hold the difficult things, and to feel a little less alone at 3am.

━━━ WHAT CARER DOES ━━━

🌿 A COMPANION THAT LISTENS
Your conversations happen on your own device, offline and private by default. CARER meets you where you are, holds the feeling first, and never hands out advice you didn't ask for.

🧡 MEMORY, IN YOUR OWN WORDS
CARER remembers what you tell it, in your own words, and gently offers it back. You never have to re-explain. Your memory is visible, editable, exportable — and always yours.

🔒 A PRIVATE SPACE FOR THE UNSAYABLE
A sealed, encrypted room for the thoughts you can't say aloud — the resentment, the anger, the wish it would just stop. Received without judgement, never re-surfaced without your say-so.

🌙 ROOM FOR GRIEF, EVEN FOR SOMEONE STILL HERE
Anticipatory grief and ambiguous loss are first-class here. CARER holds the "both/and": love and grief together, neither cancelling the other. No forced closure. And if the caring ends, CARER stays — holding the after.

🆘 HELP, ONE TAP AWAY — EVEN OFFLINE
A calm, free crisis and safeguarding route lives on every screen. It works with no signal, before sign-in, and never behind a paywall. Supporting four directions: your own safety, breaking point, fear of harm to the person you care for, and harm being done to you.

━━━ WHAT CARER IS NOT ━━━

CARER is not therapy, counselling, or a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat, assess, or monitor anyone — not you, and not the person you care for. When something needs a clinician or a crisis service, CARER says so honestly and points you there.

No burnout meters. No scores. No streaks. No guilt-trip notifications. The person you care for is never profiled, tracked, or monitored.

━━━ PRIVACY, BY DESIGN ━━━

✓ On-device by default — your words don't need to leave your phone
✓ Optional end-to-end encrypted backup — even we can't read it
✓ Cloud assistance is off unless you turn it on
✓ We never sell your data or train AI on it
✓ Export or delete everything, any time

━━━ FOR CARERS EVERYWHERE ━━━

Made by Wellnetix Ltd in the United Kingdom. Available to carers worldwide. Crisis resources inside the app are tailored to your country. The website covers the UK, the US, and 175+ countries via Find A Helpline (findahelpline.com).

CARER is for adults aged 18 and over. If you are a young carer under 18, CARER will point you to dedicated young-carer services.

─────────────────────────────────────────
⚠️ IMPORTANT: CARER is not an emergency or medical service and does not provide medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency services — 999 (UK) · 911 (US/Canada) · 112 (Europe). For crisis resources worldwide, visit carerapp.com/safety.
```

## Category

## Content Rating (IARC Self-Rating Questionnaire)

**Expected outcome: Mature 17+** in most territories.

Key questionnaire considerations:
- **Violence:** No. The app contains text references to domestic abuse and self-harm in a safeguarding context (helpline-signposting only), but no violent content or depictions.
- **Sexual content:** No.
- **Substances:** No.
- **Language:** None. App uses warm, plain language.
- **User-generated content:** Content is private to the individual user; no public social feed.
- **Sensitive topics:** Yes — mental health, emotional crisis, domestic abuse references (as safeguarding signposts). Declare this accurately.
- **Interactive elements:** User interacts with an AI companion; no social sharing, no purchases in free tier.

_Note: Mental health apps that reference self-harm or crisis situations (even for safety purposes) typically receive Mature 17+ from IARC/PEGI/ESRB. This is correct for CARER. Complete the IARC questionnaire honestly — the crisis layer is a protective feature, not content, but the system rates on declared topics._
