Welcome

A companion for you, for the long road

The first thing you see: a warm open door, a quiet promise. No forms, no quiz, no goal to set.

On your device, offline

The companion AI runs entirely on your phone. Your words can stay completely private — no server needed.

❦

Remembers in your words

CARER keeps a memory of what you've shared and surfaces it gently — in your own words, at the right moment.

◯

Crisis always one tap away

Free, offline, before sign-in. Crisis support is built into the app — always there, whatever else is happening.

No score. Never.

CARER never measures, rates, or diagnoses you. No burnout score, no mood graph, no performance for anyone to see.
