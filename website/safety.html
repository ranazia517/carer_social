How CARER helps in a hard moment

CARER's crisis and safeguarding support is designed to be there when things feel frightening — for you, or for the person you care for. It's built to be free, one tap away on every screen, and available even offline and before you've signed in. It works whether or not the companion is running.

It supports four kinds of hard moment:

  1. When you feel unsafe, or don't want to go on.
  2. When you're at breaking point and can't do this anymore.
  3. When the strain has you frightened of what you might do to the person you care for.
  4. When the person you care for is hurting you.

In every case, CARER responds without shock or judgement. It holds the feeling, never validates a harmful act, and puts the right route one tap away. It won't label anything "abuse" or "neglect," it isn't an assessor, and it will be honest that it can't contact anyone on your behalf. If you can't speak aloud because someone's nearby, it offers a text route first.

In an emergency — immediate danger to life

  • Call your local emergency number 999 · 911 · 112 999 in the UK  ·  911 in the US and Canada  ·  112 across the EU and many other countries. If you can't speak (UK), call 999 then press 55 on a mobile to use the Silent Solution.

Wherever you are — Find A Helpline

Whatever you're going through, wherever you are in the world, Find A Helpline connects you to free, confidential crisis and emotional-support services in 175+ countries. It's the global starting point if you need help and the country-specific resources below don't apply to you.

  • Find A Helpline findahelpline.com Free · Confidential · 175+ countries · By ThroughLine

In the United Kingdom

UK numbers verified 3 July 2026 against the CARER app's maintained crisis configuration.

  • Samaritans 116 123 Free, 24/7 emotional support — call any time
  • Shout (text) Text SHOUT to 85258 Free, 24/7 text-based crisis support
  • NHS 111 (mental health) 111 Press option 2 for mental health, 24/7. Or text 07860 009642
  • Carers UK helpline 0808 808 7777 Mon–Fri 9am–6pm. Free. For outside those hours, use a 24/7 line above.
  • Adult safeguarding — your local council Contact your local council Under the Care Act 2014, anyone can raise a safeguarding concern for an adult at risk.
  • National Domestic Abuse Helpline 0808 2000 247 Free, 24/7 — if someone is hurting you

In the United States

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 988 Call, text, or chat at 988lifeline.org · Free, 24/7, confidential
  • Crisis Text Line Text HOME to 741741 Free, 24/7 text-based crisis support

If you're a young carer (under 18)

CARER is for adults 18 and over — but if you're younger and caring for someone at home, you deserve just as much support. These services are here for you.

  • Childline (UK) 0800 1111 Free, 24/7, doesn't appear on the phone bill
  • Find A Helpline (worldwide) findahelpline.com Free crisis support for young people in 175+ countries

Inside the CARER app, we surface crisis and support resources for your country — so wherever you are, you'll always see what's relevant to you.
