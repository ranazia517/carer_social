01 · WHO WE ARE

The innovation studio behind the products

Wellnetix Ltd is the parent company behind NiMind and CARER. We act as a digital-health catalyst, bridging advanced technology and human-centred care — grounded in the belief that technology should serve humanity, not complicate it.

02 · OUR MISSION

Advancing wellbeing at scale

To advance human wellbeing through intelligent technologies. We build scalable, ethical, privacy-first systems that unlock deep behavioural insight — empowering individuals and professionals to make better decisions for mental health.

CARER

A Private Companion for Unpaid Family Carers

CARER is a warm, private, non-clinical AI companion for the millions of unpaid family carers — somewhere to be heard, to hold the hard feelings, and to be remembered, with free crisis support one tap away. It runs on the carer's own device by default, so what they share stays with them.

Visit CARER →
Built for carers
✓ On-device by default — private, works offline
✓ Remembers your own words — never a score or profile
✓ Free, always-on crisis & safeguarding support
✓ Made specifically for unpaid family carers
